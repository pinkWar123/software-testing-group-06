    [Task2] AI analysis + misinterpretation hunt + optimization judgment
    
    Real two-pass analysis of the live Load/Stress/Spike .jtl logs via
    analysis/compute_stats.js. First pass (ai_analysis_raw.md) reads the
    headline numbers at face value. Second pass (misinterpretation_hunt.md)
    finds 5 real misreadings cited against raw values: "0% errors" masking
    85-98% business-rejection 400s, GET/PUT "identical degradation"
    ignoring a ~5x response-payload growth confound (342KB->1.7MB) versus
    PUT's constant ~335 bytes, Spike's blended overall average hiding a
    near-instant recovery to baseline, an implied linear thread-count
    relationship that the data shows is sublinear, and a CSV-sharing
    collision artifact behind Load's 10.6% PUT success rate.
    optimization_judgment.md rules on the AI's 4 proposed optimizations
    (WAL mode feasible, index and connection pool hallucinated for this
    embedded single-writer SQLite stack, caching feasible but not
    well-targeted) and flags pagination - the fix most directly supported
    by the AI's own data - as conspicuously absent from its own proposals.
